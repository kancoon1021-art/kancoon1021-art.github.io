Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shortsunnivers Studio ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • user.info.basic: to show which TikTok account is connected (display name and open ID).
  • video.upload: to send the owner's videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • video.publish: to publish the owner's videos with a caption to the owner's TikTok account.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to post the owner's videos. The app does not collect data about any other TikTok users, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. No data is used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes all stored data.

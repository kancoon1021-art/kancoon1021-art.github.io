Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short Automation ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos and set their title, description and publish time on the owner's channels.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is signed in before uploading.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone.
  • The app does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer removes all stored data.
